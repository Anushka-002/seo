Shoes are important for people who like to go outside and exercise. Many people buy shoes online or in stores and there are lots of colors and styles to pick from. Some shoes look nice and some are comfortable. My friend has a pair that he likes a lot and wears them every weekend when he goes out. Brands make new models every year and advertise them on television and social media. It is a good idea to think about what you like before you spend money. Sometimes shoes are on sale and that can be a good time to look around the shop. Overall shoes matter and everyone should have a pair that makes them happy when they walk around town or visit family.
